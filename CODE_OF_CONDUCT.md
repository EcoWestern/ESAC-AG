# Code of conduct

ESAC-AG accepts no code contributions, but it does have issue trackers, and those are read by
people. The expectations below apply to everyone participating there, maintainers included.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical substance and civil about the person. The two are independent.
- Engage the claim, not the author. "This item is guessable, here is the shortcut" is useful.
  "This benchmark is worthless" is not wrong because it is harsh, but because it gives nothing
  to work with.
- Assume a report is made in good faith until there is evidence otherwise.
- Respect that a change may be declined. A closed issue is a design decision, not a personal
  verdict.
- Keep reports free of API keys, held-out seeds, and private evaluation material. This suite
  executes generated code, so pasted transcripts can carry content that should not be public.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments about a person's identity or background.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing or hijacking issue threads.
- Sustained disruption, including spam, repeated re-litigation of closed issues, and pressure
  campaigns aimed at individual maintainers.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, and any repository run by the project)
and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately through the channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are read by
the maintainers only.

Maintainers may warn, edit or delete comments, lock threads, or block accounts. Consequences
escalate with severity and repetition. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good
faith may face consequences determined by the other maintainers.
